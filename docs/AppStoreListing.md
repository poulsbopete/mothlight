## Description

Guide a tiny glowing moth through a moonlit forest by seeking light.

**Phototaxis controls**
- Hold or drag toward light to fly
- Collect fireflies, lantern wisps, and moonbeams
- Chain combos for bigger scores
- Manage your glow—run out and the night wins

**Built for quick sessions**
- Portrait one-thumb play
- Instant restarts
- Personal best tracking
- Optional sound and haptics

**Support the game**
- Play for free with ads between runs
- Remove ads forever with a one-time purchase
- Optional rewarded ad to continue after a close call (once per run)

No accounts required. Just you, the forest, and the glow.
